Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Information we use

CeeFusion uses your email address, display name, target exam and authentication identifiers to provide your account. Study progress, mock results, schedules and scratchpad notes are stored in your browser for the signed-in account.

AI study tutor

Questions sent to the AI study tutor, together with relevant course material, are processed by Supabase and Google Gemini to generate an answer. Do not submit passwords, financial information, government identifiers, medical records or other sensitive personal information to the tutor.

Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication and backend functions, Render and Cloudflare for website delivery, and Google Gemini for course-related AI responses. These providers may process technical data such as IP addresses and request metadata to operate and secure their services.

Security and retention

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not stored in CeeFusion source code. Browser study data remains on the device until it is cleared or the user signs out. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can request account assistance through the Contact Us section. You can also clear browser storage to remove locally stored study data.

Children and students

CeeFusion is an independent study platform. Students should avoid entering unnecessary personal information. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required by local law.
